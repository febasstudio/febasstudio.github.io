Privacy policy

Español

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Englimals app uses, what for, and what you can do about it. Englimals is for people aged 13 or over.

1. Who is responsible

Febas Studio, the studio that publishes Englimals. Contact: febasstudio@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Your pets, coins, items, lesson progress, mistakes to review and settings are stored only on your phone. They never leave it unless you turn on the cloud backup or join the leaderboard. If you delete the app, they are deleted.

3. Cloud backup (optional)

If you sign in with your Google account in Settings, “Cloud backup”, we store:

  • your account ID and email address, to know whose backup it is;
  • a copy of your game (the data in point 2) and a summary (pet names, level, coins and date), to show you when restoring it.

We only use it to store your game and give it back to you. The legal basis is that you ask us to when you turn the backup on (performance of the service). The backup is stored in Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.), on servers in the European Union; Google acts as our processor and may process account data outside the EU with the safeguards required by law (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses). Each account can only read its own backup and the connection is encrypted.

The backup is kept until you delete it. You can sign out at any time and delete the backup and the account from the app (see how to delete your account).

4. Endless mode leaderboard (optional)

If you join the leaderboard (the “Leaderboard” button in endless mode), an anonymous Google Firebase account is created, with no name or email, and we store in the cloud, visible to other players:

  • an anonymous account ID;
  • a nickname made up by the app (for example, “Swift Panda 482”; you can't type your own text);
  • the species of the pet you played with;
  • your best scores per level, for the week and all time, and the date you got them.

While you play a game we also store its level and when it started, to check that the result is possible; it is deleted when the game ends. We only use this to show the leaderboard. The legal basis is that you ask for it when joining (performance of the service). It is stored in Google Firebase, like the backup in point 3. If you later sign in with your Google account, the leaderboard moves to that account.

You can leave at any time in Settings, “Leaderboard”, “Leave the leaderboard”: your positions in all levels and the anonymous account are deleted. “Delete my backup” also deletes them.

5. Microphone

It is only used while you press the microphone button in the speaking exercises. Your phone's system (Google or Apple) recognises your speech, on the phone itself whenever possible. Englimals never stores or sends your voice.

6. Reminders

Your pets' reminders are scheduled by the phone itself. We don't send anything to any server for them. You can turn them off in Settings.

7. Ads and purchases

The app has no ads or purchases right now. If they are added, Google AdMob and your phone's store will handle them; we will update this policy first and ask for your consent when the law requires it.

8. What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with anyone for advertising, or use it for anything other than making the app work.

9. Children

Englimals is not meant for children under 13. If we learn that a child under that age has given us data, we will delete it.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your data, and to receive a copy of it, by writing to febasstudio@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. If you disagree with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is important, let you know in the app.
