Privacy policy

Español

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Englimals app uses, what for, and what you can do about it. Englimals is for people aged 13 or over.

1. Who is responsible

Febas Studio, the studio that publishes Englimals. Contact: febasstudio@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Your pets, coins, items, lesson progress, mistakes to review and settings are stored only on your phone. They never leave it unless you turn on the cloud backup or join the leaderboard. If you delete the app, they are deleted.

3. Cloud backup (optional)

If you sign in with your Google account in Settings, “Cloud backup”, we store:

  • your account ID and email address, to know whose backup it is;
  • a copy of your game (the data in point 2) and a summary (pet names, level, coins and date), to show you when restoring it.

We only use it to store your game and give it back to you. The legal basis is that you ask us to when you turn the backup on (performance of the service). The backup is stored in Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.), on servers in the European Union; Google acts as our processor and may process account data outside the EU with the safeguards required by law (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses). Each account can only read its own backup and the connection is encrypted.

The backup is kept until you delete it. You can sign out at any time and delete the backup and the account from the app (see how to delete your account).

4. Endless mode leaderboard (optional)

If you join the leaderboard (the “Leaderboard” button in endless mode), we store in Google Firebase, visible to other players:

  • your account ID: that of an anonymous Google Firebase account (no name or email) created when you join or, if you have signed in with Google, that of your Google account (then no anonymous account is created). That ID is visible in the leaderboard data, but it is not a name or an email;
  • a nickname made up by the app (for example, “Swift Panda 482”; you can't type your own text);
  • the species of the pet you played with;
  • your best scores per level, for the week and all time, and the date you got them.

While you play a game we also store its level and the time it started, to check that the result is possible; they are deleted when the game ends. We only use this to show the leaderboard. The legal basis is that you ask for it when joining (performance of the service). It is stored in Google Firebase, like the backup in point 3. If you later sign in with your Google account, the leaderboard moves to that account.

You can leave at any time in Settings, “Leaderboard”, “Leave the leaderboard”: your positions in all levels and the anonymous account are deleted; if you have signed in with Google, that account's positions are deleted. If you sign out of Google, you leave the leaderboard on that phone, but your positions stay with your Google account, like the backup: they are deleted with “Leave the leaderboard” while you are signed in with it, or with “Delete my backup”.

5. Friends (optional)

If you're on the leaderboard, you can add friends with a 6-character code that is created when you open “Friends”. We store in Google Firebase, under your account ID:

  • your friend code (anyone who has it can send you a request);
  • your profile, which only the friends you have accepted can see: your nickname, the species and stage of your main pet, your English level, your streak, your lessons this week, your number of achievements and when it was updated;
  • friend requests, with your nickname and pet (seen by whoever receives them);
  • your friends list;
  • the encouragements you send: one of 6 fixed messages from the app, with its date.

There is no free text and no names. Your endless mode scores are already public on the leaderboard (point 4). The legal basis is that you ask for it by using “Friends” (performance of the service). Leaving the leaderboard or “Delete my backup” deletes your code, your profile, your requests, your friendships (on both lists) and your encouragements. If you sign out of Google, your friends stay with that account, like the leaderboard.

6. Microphone

It is only used while you press the microphone button in the speaking exercises. Your phone's system (Google or Apple) recognises your speech, on the phone itself whenever possible. Englimals never stores or sends your voice.

7. Reminders

Your pets' reminders are scheduled by the phone itself. We don't send anything to any server for them. You can turn them off in Settings.

8. Ads and purchases

Ads (optional). They only show if you choose to watch one in exchange for a reward (a food or a wheel spin). No ads show up on their own. They are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited), which receives device data (such as the model, the system, the language and the IP address) and, on Android, your phone's advertising ID, to show them, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent first with Google's form (legal basis: your consent); you can change or withdraw it at any time in Settings, “Ads”, “Ad privacy”. On iPhone we don't ask for tracking permission: we don't track you across apps and AdMob doesn't receive the iPhone's advertising ID. On Android you can reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone's settings. More information: how Google uses data in ads.

Purchases (optional). You can buy coin packs. So you don't lose them, you need to sign in with Google first (point 3). The purchase is made by your phone's store (Google Play or App Store) under its terms. RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., in the USA, with the safeguards required by law) keeps the purchase history (product, date and purchase ID) with the ID of your Google account in the app (the same one as the cloud backup), so your coins are delivered without losing or repeating them (legal basis: performance of the service). Your game records the purchases already delivered (it travels in the backup) and the phone itself records the purchases started on it: if a purchase is left unfinished (for example, a pending payment), it is delivered later only on the phone where it was made. We never see your payment details. Coins never buy lessons and wheel tickets can only be paid with coins earned by playing.

9. What we don't do

We don't sell your data or use it for anything other than making the app work. Apart from the ads you choose to watch (point 8), we don't share anything with anyone for advertising.

10. Children

Englimals is not meant for children under 13. If we learn that a child under that age has given us data, we will delete it.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your data, and to receive a copy of it, by writing to febasstudio@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. If you disagree with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is important, let you know in the app.
