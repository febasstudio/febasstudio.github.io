Delete your account and data

Español

Englimals only stores data outside your phone if you turned on the cloud backup or joined the leaderboard. This is how to delete it:

From the app (immediately)

  1. Open Englimals and go to Settings.
  2. Under “Cloud backup”, tap “Delete my backup”.
  3. Confirm with “Delete”.

Your cloud backup and your Englimals cloud account are deleted. The game on your phone is not touched.

For the leaderboard: in Settings, “Leaderboard”, tap “Leave the leaderboard” and confirm. Your positions in all levels are deleted and, if you didn't sign in with Google, the anonymous account too. If you signed in with Google, that account's positions are deleted: do it while signed in (if you signed out, sign in with it again) or use “Delete my backup”, which also deletes them.

For friends: leaving the leaderboard or “Delete my backup” also deletes your friend code, your profile, your requests, your friendships (on both lists) and your encouragements.

For purchases: the purchase history kept by RevenueCat is not deleted from the app (it makes sure you don't lose what you paid for). If you want it deleted, write to us as explained below. Your phone's store keeps its own receipts under its terms.

Without the app

Write to febasstudio@gmail.com from the email of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Englimals account”. We will delete it within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

Your game backup, its summary, your account ID and your email. We keep nothing afterwards. If you were on the leaderboard, also your positions, your nickname and your pet's species. If you had friends, also your code, your profile, your requests, your friendships and your encouragements.

Data on your phone

It is deleted when you uninstall the app.
