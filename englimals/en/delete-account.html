Delete your account and data

Español

Englimals only stores data outside your phone if you turned on the cloud backup. This is how to delete it:

From the app (immediately)

  1. Open Englimals and go to Settings.
  2. Under “Cloud backup”, tap “Delete my backup”.
  3. Confirm with “Delete”.

Your cloud backup and your Englimals cloud account are deleted. The game on your phone is not touched.

Without the app

Write to febasstudio@gmail.com from the email of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Englimals account”. We will delete it within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

Your game backup, its summary, your account ID and your email. We keep nothing afterwards.

Data on your phone

It is deleted when you uninstall the app.
